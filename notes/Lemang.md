## In one sentence

Lemang is glutinous rice and coconut milk stuffed into a banana-leaf-lined bamboo tube and roasted beside an open fire for three to five hours, until it comes out as a smoky log of rice that you slice like a cake and eat with rendang.

## The story

Nobody knows exactly how old lemang is. The bamboo-over-fire method belongs to the forest, whereby the Orang Asli (the indigenous people of Peninsular Malaysia) and early Malay communities cooked in a green bamboo tube because it was the pot that grew by the river. The Minangkabau of West Sumatra, who settled Negeri Sembilan, call it lamang and eat it with tapai (fermented glutinous rice), which I have honestly never tried. It even turns up in Marah Rusli's 1922 novel Siti Nurbaya, where a character is poisoned by one (so by 1922 it was common enough to be a murder weapon).

Today in Malaysia, lemang means Hari Raya. It appears on the roadside in the last week of Ramadan, and Lemang To'Ki in Bentong, Pahang, sells a medium stick for RM10 and a large one for RM13. In our case, the Raya lemang always comes from a roadside stall on the way back to kampung and is gone before the ketupat is out of the pot.

## Why it is the way it is

Firstly, the bamboo is a specific one. Buluh lemang (Schizostachyum brachycladum) has a wall only 3 to 5 mm thick, so it heats through quickly and splits cleanly with one hit of a parang, and because it is green and full of water it does not catch fire while leaning on the flames. This is a case of the tool choosing the recipe, as opposed to the other way round.

Secondly, the banana leaf is not decoration. Glutinous rice is high in amylopectin, the branched starch that makes it sticky, and without the leaf it would weld itself to the bamboo.

Thirdly, the slow cook beside the fire makes the texture. Over hours the coconut milk reduces into the rice and the outside grows a chewy skin. A pressure cooker does glutinous rice in 20 minutes, but it comes out as sticky rice, not lemang.

Lastly, why Raya? One person cannot cut the bamboo, tend the fire and turn 30 tubes for four hours, so it became a gotong-royong (communal work) dish for the one time of year the whole kampung is home.
