## In one sentence

Rendang is beef simmered for hours in coconut milk and spices until almost all the liquid cooks away, which is not a stylistic choice but a preservation method invented by the Minangkabau people of West Sumatra to keep meat edible for weeks without refrigeration, and that same long, patient reduction is exactly what CNN Travel's readers voted the world's most delicious food in a 2011 poll.

## The story

Truth be told, rendang did not start in Malaysia at all, though the dish is now claimed proudly across both countries. It originates with the Minangkabau ethnic group of West Sumatra, Indonesia, and travelled across the Malay world through Minangkabau migration and trade, especially the merantau tradition, whereby young Minangkabau men traditionally left home to seek their fortune elsewhere, carrying the dish with them into what is now Malaysia, Singapore and beyond.

The dish's fame outside Southeast Asia grew sharply after that 2011 CNN Travel readers' poll, which sparked a genuine cross-border pride dispute, whereby both Indonesia and Malaysia claimed rendang as their own national dish loudly enough that Indonesia formally lodged rendang, alongside several other dishes, for UNESCO intangible cultural heritage recognition specifically to settle the origin question.

## Why it is the way it is

Under the hood, rendang's defining trait is the cooking process itself, not any single spice. Beef is simmered slowly in coconut milk with a spice paste, lemongrass, galangal, turmeric leaf, chilli and other aromatics, for anywhere from two to four hours, until the coconut milk breaks down into oil and the liquid reduces almost entirely away, leaving the meat coated in a dark, dry, intensely flavoured paste rather than sitting in a sauce.

Firstly, this reduction is why properly made rendang keeps for days without refrigeration even in tropical heat, since almost all the water content that would let bacteria grow has cooked off, a genuinely practical invention before refrigeration existed at all. Secondly, the colour and dryness signal how rendang was cooked, since a wetter, saucier version, closer to a curry, is technically a different, earlier stage of the same dish called kalio, and true rendang is what kalio becomes after several more hours over the fire.

## The variants

| Name | What changes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Rendang daging (beef) | The classic, most internationally known version |
| Rendang ayam (chicken) | Lighter, cooks faster, common at home |
| Kalio | The earlier, wetter, saucier stage before full rendang reduction |
| Rendang Minang (original) | Drier, darker, spicier than most Malaysian versions |

## Where I met it

In our case, rendang for me means Hari Raya specifically, cooked the day before in a pot big enough for the whole extended family, and the smell of it simmering for hours is as much a part of the memory as the taste itself, which is probably true for most Malay households during that one week of the year.
