## In one sentence

TikTok won the short video format Vine had already proven, by adding a recommendation feed that needed no follows to work and merging with a rival, musical.ly, to get 100 million users in one move, and that is how a Chinese app nobody outside China had heard of in 2016 became the most downloaded app in the world by 2021.

## Situation

ByteDance, a Beijing company founded by Zhang Yiming in 2012, launched Douyin in China in September 2016 and the international version, TikTok, in September 2017. In November 2017 ByteDance bought musical.ly, a lip-sync app with about 100 million users, mostly teenagers in the US, for a reported \$1 billion, and in August 2018 it merged musical.ly into TikTok, carrying every one of those accounts over in one step.

By 2020 TikTok had passed 2 billion downloads worldwide, and in 2021 Cloudflare and App Annie both ranked it the most visited or most downloaded app of the year, ahead of Google and Facebook.

## Core problem

The one tension at hand is that every short video app before TikTok, Vine included, showed a feed built from who you followed, so a new account started with an empty feed and no reason to stay. TikTok's For You page instead started every viewer on machine-picked videos from strangers, meaning day one already felt full.

## What happened

Firstly, the algorithm did the work follows used to do. The For You page ranks videos by watch time and completion rate on that one video, not by follower count, so a first-time poster with zero followers can still reach millions if the video holds attention, which flattened the usual gap between a new account and an established one.

Secondly, the musical.ly merger solved the cold start problem instantly. As opposed to Vine, which had to grow its creator base from nothing, TikTok inherited 100 million accounts and their video habits (lip-syncing, dancing, short skits) in a single day in August 2018.

Thirdly, the format stayed loose. Videos ranged from 15 seconds up to 10 minutes by 2022, sound clips and effects were built into the app rather than added after, and a creator fund launched in 2020 paid out for views, which is the exact thing Vine never gave its creators.

Lastly, competitors chased it rather than the other way round. Instagram launched Reels in August 2020 and YouTube launched Shorts in September 2020, both direct copies of the format, which is usually a sign a platform has already won the category.

## Lessons

To begin with, the algorithm mattered more than the follow graph. TikTok proved a feed can manufacture an audience for a stranger's first post, which no app before it trusted itself to do. In addition, buying the audience beat growing it. The musical.ly deal handed TikTok in one step what would have taken years of organic growth, and Vine, without a deal like that, never found its Instagram.

For me, I feel the clearest sign of TikTok's win is that its two biggest rivals stopped competing on their own terms and copied its layout exactly, feed and all.
