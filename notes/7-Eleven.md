## In one sentence

Convenience stores are everywhere precisely because they are built around solving a genuinely different problem from a supermarket, not offering the widest selection or the lowest price, but guaranteeing that whatever a customer wants right now is within a short walk and available at almost any hour, a promise that lets them charge noticeably higher prices without losing customers who are paying for that convenience specifically.

## What it does

7-Eleven operates and franchises convenience stores worldwide, selling a limited but carefully chosen range of snacks, drinks, prepared food and everyday essentials, typically open 24 hours, a operating model built entirely around speed and accessibility rather than breadth of selection or price competitiveness.

## History

| Year | Milestone |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1927 | The chain's predecessor opens in Dallas, Texas, initially selling ice, milk and eggs from an ice house |
| 1946 | The store adopts the name "7-Eleven," reflecting its then-unusual operating hours of 7am to 11pm |
| 1963 | 7-Eleven becomes one of the first chains to operate around the clock, 24 hours a day |
| 1991 | Japan's Ito-Yokado group acquires a majority stake in the struggling American parent company |
| 2005 | Seven & I Holdings, the Japanese parent group, is formally established, headquartered in Japan despite the brand's American origin |

## How it makes money

7-Eleven earns revenue through direct company-operated store sales and, for the large majority of its locations, franchise fees paid by independent operators, and its profit margin depends heavily on higher-margin prepared food and private-label products rather than the lower-margin packaged snacks and drinks that make up much of visible shelf space.

## Products

7-Eleven's approach varies significantly by region, its Japanese stores in particular are widely regarded as some of the most sophisticated convenience retail operations globally, offering genuinely high-quality fresh food restocked multiple times daily, a standard that has shaped consumer expectations for convenience stores across much of Asia, Malaysia's own 7-Eleven network included.
