## In one sentence

A modern bank like Maybank makes money mainly through one deceptively simple mechanism, borrowing money cheaply from depositors and lending it out more expensively to borrowers, with the difference between those two rates, called the net interest margin, forming the core of its profit even before counting fees from cards, wealth management or investment banking on top.

## What it does

Maybank operates as Malaysia's largest bank by assets, offering retail and commercial banking, Islamic banking, insurance and investment banking services across Malaysia and several other Southeast Asian markets, functioning as a genuinely broad financial services group rather than a narrowly focused retail bank alone.

## How it makes money

Beyond the core interest margin between deposits and loans, Maybank earns substantial fee-based revenue from services like wealth management, credit cards and investment banking, and its Islamic banking arm, structured around profit-sharing and asset-backed financing rather than conventional interest under Shariah principles, has become one of the largest Islamic banking operations in the world by assets, reflecting Malaysia's broader position as a global hub for Islamic finance.

## Products

Maybank's retail banking products span ordinary savings accounts, mortgages and credit cards, but its Maybank2u digital banking platform, launched relatively early compared to regional peers, has become one of the most widely used online banking services in Malaysia, reflecting a broader industry shift toward digital-first banking that traditional branch-based banks have had to adapt to compete with newer digital-only challengers.
