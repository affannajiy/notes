## In one sentence

To talk rally, I need to know that it is one car against the clock on a closed real road, the car classes from Rally1 down to Rally4, a dozen words, the three teams, the Group B story, and that Elfyn Evans goes into the final round in Sardinia leading by 17 points.

## How a rally works

Rally is defined as a race against the clock on closed public roads, one car at a time, as opposed to circuit racing where everyone starts together. A rally runs over three or four days and is split into 15 to 20 special stages (the timed, closed sections), linked by liaisons (normal roads, speed limits and all). The fastest total time wins. Truth be told, the part I love most is the co-driver, who reads pace notes (a coded description of every corner, written during the recce) out loud at 150 km/h while the driver trusts every word blind.

## Car classes

## Terms

| Term | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| Special stage (SS) | A timed, closed section of road |
| Liaison | The public road between stages, driven legally |
| Recce | Reconnaissance, the slow drive before the rally to write pace notes |
| Pace notes | "Left 4 over crest, don't cut" - the co-driver's script for every corner |
| Service park | The pit area, with strict time limits for repairs |
| Power Stage | The last stage, with bonus points for the five fastest |
| Super Sunday | Extra points for the best Sunday total, added in 2024 |
| Surfaces | Gravel, tarmac and snow, and each needs a different setup |
| Scandinavian flick | Swinging the car away from a corner first to throw the rear round |
| Group B | The 1982 to 1986 era of 500 hp monsters, banned after a fatal crash |
| Homologation | A rally car must be based on a road car the maker actually sells |

## The history in three eras

Firstly, Group B, from 1982 to 1986, was the wild one: the Audi Quattro brought four-wheel drive, then the Lancia Delta S4 and Peugeot 205 T16 went even further, with crowds standing on the road. On 2 May 1986 at the Tour de Corse, Henri Toivonen and his co-driver Sergio Cresto died when their Delta S4 went off a ravine, and Group B was banned within hours. Secondly, Group A replaced it with 300 hp road-based cars, which gave us the 1990s heroes I grew up watching in games: the Subaru Impreza of Colin McRae and the Mitsubishi Lancer Evo of Tommi Mäkinen. Lastly, the modern era is the Sébastien Loeb and Sébastien Ogier era, whereby the two Frenchmen now share the record at nine titles each.

## The grid now

Three teams run Rally1 cars: Toyota Gazoo Racing (GR Yaris), Hyundai (i20 N) and M-Sport Ford (Puma). Toyota won its sixth manufacturers' title in a row at Rally Chile, 10 to 13 Sep 2026.

| | Drivers' title |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2025 champion | Sébastien Ogier, Toyota, 9th title, by 4 points over Elfyn Evans |
| 2026 so far | Evans 230, Sami Pajari 213, Oliver Solberg 209 after Chile (Round 12) |
| Decider | Rally Italia Sardegna, 1 to 4 Oct 2026. Saudi Arabia was dropped |

Kalle Rovanperä, the two-time champion, left for Super Formula in 2026. Pajari won three rallies in a row (Estonia, Finland, Paraguay), and Solberg is the son of 2003 champion Petter Solberg.

## The Malaysian connection

In our case, the proudest moment is Karamjit Singh, the "Flying Sikh", who became the first Asian to win a world rally title: the 2002 Production World Rally Championship (PWRC, the class for showroom-based cars), driving for Proton Pert, a team backed by Proton and PETRONAS. Rally Japan is the closest WRC round to us.
