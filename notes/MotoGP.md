## In one sentence

MotoGP is the top class of motorcycle Grand Prix racing, run by six manufacturers on prototype bikes that touch 360 km/h, and unlike Formula 1 the rider sits exposed on top of the machine with nothing but leather and a helmet between him and the tarmac.

## What it is

MotoGP is the premier class of the FIM Grand Prix motorcycle racing world championship, organised commercially by Dorna Sports since 1992. A season runs around 20 races (Grands Prix) across five continents, and since 2023 every round has carried a Saturday sprint race half the length of Sunday's main race, doubling the points on offer. Below MotoGP sit two feeder classes on the same race weekend, Moto2 and Moto3, which use standardised or smaller engines so young riders can move up the ladder.

## Types (the classes)

| Class | Engine | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| MotoGP | 1000cc four-cylinder prototype | Top class, factory-built bikes |
| Moto2 | 765cc Triumph triple, same for all teams | Mid tier, chassis differ, engine does not |
| Moto3 | 250cc single, same for all teams | Entry tier for teenage riders |
| MotoE | Electric, one make | Support class since 2019 |

## History in moments

Giacomo Agostini won 15 world titles across classes between 1966 and 1975, still the most of anyone. Valentino Rossi, an Italian rider, won 9 titles between 1997 and 2009 and is widely credited with turning MotoGP into a global spectacle through personality as much as speed. Marc Marquez, a Spaniard, has taken the record for most premier class wins from Rossi's era into the 2020s, riding for Repsol Honda then Ducati.

Ducati, an Italian manufacturer, dominated the mid 2020s so thoroughly that the sport introduced rules from 2027 specifically to slow it down, including banning ride height devices and cutting engine capacity from 1000cc to 850cc, the biggest technical shake-up since the switch from two-stroke to four-stroke engines in 2002.

## The Malaysian connection

The Sepang International Circuit near Kuala Lumpur has hosted a MotoGP round almost every year since 1999, making Malaysia one of the longest-running stops on the calendar outside Europe. Malaysian rider Hafizh Syahrin raced in MotoGP itself for the Tech3 team in 2018 and 2019, the first Malaysian to reach the top class.

## What is happening now

From 2027 MotoGP moves to smaller 850cc engines and drops several aerodynamic and electronic aids, a deliberate reset after bikes had grown fast enough that circuits built for an earlier era were running out of margin for error.
