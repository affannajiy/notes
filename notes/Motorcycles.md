## In one sentence

To talk bikes with a rider, I need the six bike types, a dozen words, the big brands and who is winning MotoGP right now, and then I just ask them what they ride.

## Bike types

The family is mostly decided by how you sit on it.

| Type | How you sit | Seen in Malaysia |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Underbone / kapchai | Upright, step-through frame | Yamaha Y15ZR, Honda RS-X |
| Scooter | Upright, feet flat, automatic | Yamaha NVX 155 |
| Naked | Upright, no fairing (the plastic shell) | Kawasaki Z900 |
| Sport / superbike | Leaning forward, feet high | Yamaha R15, Ducati Panigale V4 |
| Cruiser | Leaning back, feet forward | Harley-Davidson, Honda Rebel 500 |
| Adventure | Tall, upright, big tank, luggage | BMW GS |

## Terms

| Term | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| cc | Engine size. A kapchai is 150cc, a superbike 1,000cc |
| Single, twin, inline-4 | Cylinder count. Single thumps, V-twin rumbles, inline-4 screams |
| Torque vs horsepower | Torque is the push pulling away, horsepower the top speed. Cruisers sell torque |
| 2-stroke vs 4-stroke | 2-stroke is light, fast and smoky (Yamaha RXZ); everything new is 4-stroke |
| ABS | Anti-lock brakes, stops the wheels skidding under hard braking |
| Quickshifter | Change gear without the clutch |
| Countersteering | Push the right bar forward to turn right. Everyone does it, few can explain it |
| ATGATT | All The Gear, All The Time |
| Kapchai | Malaysian word for an underbone, 110 to 150cc |
| Mat rempit | Illegal street racers, usually on modified kapchai |
| Konvoi | A group ride, often a Sunday morning out of KL |
| Big bike | Above 250cc. Needs the full Class B licence; B2 stops at 250cc |

## Brands

Yamaha is the one to know first: 35.4% of Malaysian motorcycle sales in 2025, out of about 670,000 bikes.

| Group | Brands |
| :--- | :--- |
| Japanese Big Four | Honda (Super Cub, RS-X), Yamaha (R1, Y15ZR), Suzuki (Hayabusa), Kawasaki (Ninja) |
| European | Ducati (Panigale), BMW (GS), KTM (Duke), Triumph (Bonneville), Aprilia (RSV4) |
| American | Harley-Davidson, the cruiser brand |
| Local and rising | Modenas (national bike since 1995, Kawasaki owns 30%), CFMoto and QJ Motor from China |

## Racing

MotoGP is the Formula 1 of bikes, prototypes you cannot buy. WSBK (World Superbike) races bikes you can buy in a shop.

| | MotoGP | WSBK |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2025 champion | Marc Márquez, Ducati, 7th premier title | Toprak Razgatlıoğlu, BMW, 3rd title |
| 2026 so far | Jorge Martín (Aprilia) leads Márquez by 12 points after Austria, 20 Sep | Nicolò Bulega (Ducati) leads by 146 after Magny-Cours, 6 Sep |

In our case, the Malaysian round is at Sepang, 30 Oct to 1 Nov 2026, confirmed on the calendar until 2031. Hafizh Syahrin became the first Southeast Asian in MotoGP in 2018.

## Conversation starters

| Ask | Follow up with |
| :--- | :--- |
| What do you ride? | "Single or twin?" |
| What was your first bike? | "Kapchai?" Most Malaysian riders started on one |
| Where do you ride on weekends? | "Genting or Bukit Tinggi? Konvoi or solo?" |
| Going to Sepang this year? | "Márquez or Martín?" |
