Things I want to keep. A hub for general knowledge I read, in notes small enough to read in
2 to 3 minutes. Only notes marked `publish: true` appear here; use the explorer on the left,
search (`Ctrl+K`, `⌘K` on Mac), the graph on the right, or the dice for a random one.

Every note sits in one folder. The kind is a tag, and the "Latest by kind" board links to each one.

- **Hubs** - one subject, with enough to hold a conversation about it
- **Companies** - one company: what it does, how it makes money, what surprised me
- **Takes** - one claim people disagree on: both sides, where I land, what would change my mind
- **Whys** - one "why" question about the world, answered in my own words
- **Cases** - one real event or decision, analysed rather than retold
- **Food** - one dish, ingredient or cuisine and the story behind it
