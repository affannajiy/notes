One short note per weekday. Different theme each day.

```
Monday    - Company   "How does this company work?"        Companies/  templates/company.md
Tuesday   - Take      "What's my take on this?"            Takes/      templates/take.md
Wednesday - Why       "Why does this happen / exist?"      Topics/     templates/topic.md
Thursday  - Case      "What happened, and why?"            Cases/      templates/case-study.md
Friday    - Food      "What's interesting about this food?" Food/       templates/food.md
```

- Monday: one company. What it does, how it makes money, competitors, edge, recent news, what surprised me. Learning note, not a report.
- Tuesday: one claim or question people disagree on. Steelman both sides, say where I land, say what would change my mind. Can be light or weird.
- Wednesday: one genuine "why" question about the world. Investigate, end with an explanation in my own words.
- Thursday: one real event, failure, success, controversy or decision. Analysis, not retelling.
- Friday: one food subject. Dish, ingredient, cuisine, history, science, business. Not a recipe.

Every note gets `[[links]]` in Related. That is what makes the graph useful.

## Weekend

No new files. No summary. Active recall only.

1. Open graph view, look at the week's 5 new nodes.
2. Before opening each note, try to recall from memory what it said and how it connects to the others.
3. Only open a note if recall fails.
4. If a real connection between two of the week's notes is not already a `[[link]]`, add it now. That is the only editing on the weekend, and only in existing notes.

A written summary would just repeat what the graph and links already show. Pulling it out of my head is the part that makes it stick.
